Cloak of Resistance

Source Ultimate Equipment pg. 264, PRPG Core Rulebook pg. 507
Aura faint abjuration; CL 5th
Slot shoulders; Price 1,000 gp ( +1), 4,000 gp ( +2), 9,000 gp ( +3), 16,000 gp ( +4), 25,000 gp ( +5); Weight 1 lb.

Description

Flecks of silver or steel are often sown amid the fabric of these magical cloaks. This garment offers magical protection in the form of a +1 to +5 resistance bonus on all saving throws (Fortitude, Reflex, and Will).

Construction

Requirements Craft Wondrous Item, resistance, creator's caster level must be at least three times the cloak's bonus; Cost 500 gp (+1), 2,000 gp (+2), 4,500 gp (+3), 8,000 gp (+4), 12,500 gp (+5)
