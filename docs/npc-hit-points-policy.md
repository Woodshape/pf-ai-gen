# NPC hit-point policy

User-approved house rule; not a claim about Pathfinder's printed average-HP convention.

- Basic NPC: every class is an NPC class (for example Expert or Warrior).
- Heroic NPC: at least one class is a PC class (for example Bard or Fighter).
- Use the same derived category as the basic/heroic NPC gear budget. Selecting a different ability array or buying extra gear does not change class category.
- Basic: average each Hit Die and round that individual average down, including the first die. No maximized die.
- Heroic: maximize the first Hit Die; average each later die and round that individual average up.
- Add Constitution once per Hit Die.
- For mixed NPC/PC-class progressions, the NPC is heroic as a whole. The first progression entry supplies the maximized first die, even when that entry is an NPC class. Later dice all use heroic rounding.
- This policy applies to the owning class-based NPC, not to separately sourced companion snapshots or Simple Monster arrays.

Examples:

| NPC | Calculation | HP |
|---|---|---:|
| Warrior 3, Con 12 (basic) | 3 × (5 + 1) | 18 |
| Bard 1, Con 13 (heroic; Warchanter) | 8 + 1 | 9 |
| Bard 2, Con 12 (heroic) | 8 + 5 + 2 | 15 |
| Sorcerer 6, Con 12 (heroic; Cinder) | 6 + 5 × 4 + 6 | 32 |
| Ranger 4/Rogue 2, Con 12 (heroic; Kiramor) | 10 + 3 × 6 + 2 × 5 + 6 | 44 |

Kiramor's printed 39 HP is retained as a source reference, not rewritten to match this house rule. The engine's previous 41 HP used a different convention: keep fractional die averages and floor only the final total.
